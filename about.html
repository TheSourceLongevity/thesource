About Us

Dedicated to helping you live longer, healthier, and more vibrant lives through personalized regenerative medicine.

Dr. Jennifer Hayes

Founder & Medical Director

Dr. Jennifer Hayes founded The Source Longevity after spending over a decade in traditional medicine and witnessing firsthand the limitations of a symptom-focused healthcare system. She saw patients struggling with fatigue, weight gain, and declining vitality—issues often dismissed as "normal aging"—and knew there had to be a better way.

After extensive training in functional medicine, hormone optimization, and regenerative therapies, Dr. Hayes opened The Source with a simple mission: to help patients not just live longer, but thrive at every stage of life. Her approach combines cutting-edge science with personalized care, treating each patient as a unique individual rather than a collection of symptoms.

"I believe everyone deserves to feel their best, regardless of age. With the right interventions and support, we can dramatically improve quality of life and extend healthspan—the years we spend in optimal health."
