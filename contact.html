Frequently Asked Questions

Your initial consultation is a comprehensive 60-90 minute appointment where we review your complete medical history, discuss your health goals, and perform a thorough evaluation. We'll order advanced diagnostic testing and create a preliminary treatment plan tailored to your needs. This is also an opportunity for you to ask questions and learn more about our approach to longevity medicine.
As a specialized longevity practice, we operate on a direct-pay model and do not bill insurance directly. However, we provide detailed superbills that you can submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement, depending on your plan's out-of-network benefits. Many of our patients use HSA/FSA accounts to cover their treatments. Our membership program offers significant value for those seeking comprehensive, ongoing care.
Results vary depending on the treatment and individual factors. Some patients notice improvements in energy and sleep within the first few weeks, while hormone optimization typically shows significant benefits within 4-8 weeks. Regenerative therapies may take 2-3 months to show full effects. We monitor your progress closely with regular follow-ups and lab work to ensure optimal outcomes.
Patient safety is our highest priority. All our treatments are evidence-based and administered under the supervision of board-certified physicians. We use bioidentical hormones, FDA-approved medications, and rigorously tested regenerative products. Before starting any treatment, we conduct thorough evaluations and ongoing monitoring to ensure safety and efficacy. We openly discuss potential risks and benefits so you can make informed decisions.

There's no "right" age to start optimizing your health. We see patients ranging from their 30s to their 80s. In general, proactive optimization starting in your late 30s to early 40s can help prevent age-related decline before it becomes significant. However, patients of any age can benefit from our services—whether you're looking to optimize performance, address specific symptoms, or simply age more gracefully.
